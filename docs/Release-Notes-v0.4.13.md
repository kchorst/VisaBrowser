# VisaBrowser v0.4.13

Sprint 1 adds 57 sourced notes to **Border and baggage rules** on individual
destination results and itinerary country cards for France, Spain, the United
States, China, Italy, Türkiye, Mexico, the United Kingdom, Japan and Germany.
Each note has a source link, check date and scope.

- Compare EU cash declarations, food restrictions and temporary admission of
  personal drones. Check Spain's published 2026 proof-of-funds amounts.
- Find the UK digital permission guidance and China's arrival card and APEC
  Business Travel Card guidance.
- Check power-bank carriage, gate-checked bags and carrier policies. Refer to
  separate mainland China domestic aviation requirements.
- Check Mexico's vaping import restrictions and documentation for covered drone
  imports, and Japan's procedures for controlled medicines.

The new notes are destination guidance. Passport comparisons and saved visa
snapshots continue to use the visa rules and data for the selected travelers.
Use the country link to Travel-Prep for local activities and restrictions.

The project tracks 350 country-category checks. One is recorded as a verified
conditional restriction, 348 retain scoped or partial evidence, and Mexico's
current local visitor curfew check remains unverified. Research gaps are retained
in the project records. See the [Sprint 1 specification](verification/Travel-Restrictions-Sprint-1-Spec.md).
