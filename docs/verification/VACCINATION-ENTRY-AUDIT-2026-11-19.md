# Required vaccination certificates for entry — initial audit, 19 November 2026

## Scope

Review African passport profiles first, then Asia, South and Central America, and the remaining profiles. Evaluate the itinerary independently of passport nationality. A destination-wide rule applies to any covered traveler. Conditional rules depend on the authority's defined recent travel, transit, age, residency, travel date or purpose. Record the certificate required at entry, exceptions, and the official source. Flag unclear rules for confirmation. Advice-only vaccinations are outside the required certificate list.

## First findings

| Destination or rule | Finding | Authority |
|---|---|---|
| Ghana | Display a destination-wide yellow fever certificate notice for covered arrivals aged 9 months and older. Only six of 201 Ghana source rows contain a vaccination reference. | [Ghana Immigration](https://evisa.immigration.gov.gh/applicant-guide.html) · [CDC destination guidance](https://wwwnc.cdc.gov/travel/destinations/traveler/none/Ghana) |
| Kenya | Kenya requires proof for arrivals from places with yellow fever transmission risk. Prompt for recent route regardless of nationality, including travelers exempt from the eTA. Confirm the exact departure and transit rules with Port Health. | [Kenya Port Health](https://porthealth.health.go.ke/faqs) |
| South Africa | Health protocols require the ICVP for travelers over one year old arriving from a risk country. South African consular guidance also identifies airport transits exceeding 12 hours in risk countries. Use the destination's country list and confirm exemptions. | [South African Health Department](https://www.health.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Protocols-for-Travellers.pdf) · [South African High Commission](https://dirco.gov.za/canberra/yellow-fever/) |
| Singapore | Yellow fever certificate required after relevant visits in the preceding six days, including airport transit exceeding 12 hours in a listed risk country. | [Singapore Immigration](https://www.ica.gov.sg/information-and-e-services/entry-and-departure/entering-singapore/entry-requirements/yellow-fever-vaccination-certificate) |
| India | India's passenger advisory calls for original yellow fever proof for covered arrivals within six days of departure from a listed endemic country. Check the applicable travel and age exceptions in the advisory. | [India Ministry of Health](https://www.mohfw.gov.in/sites/default/files/Advisory%20for%20Passengers%20Coming%20to%20India%20from%20Yellow%20Fever%20Endemics%20Countries_0.pdf) |
| Brazil | The health ministry says Brazil does not require vaccination proof for inbound entry. The outbound itinerary may trigger a certificate requirement elsewhere. | [Brazil Ministry of Health](https://www.gov.br/saude/pt-br/vacinacao/viajantes/vacinacao-para-os-viajantes) |
| Ecuador | The ministry removed the certificate requirement for arrivals from Colombia, Peru, Bolivia and Brazil in August 2025. Its announcement retains vaccination advice. Recheck the current route before removing older app wording. | [Ecuador Ministry of Public Health](https://www.salud.gob.ec/el-ministerio-de-salud-publica-de-Ecuador-modifica-la-obligatoriedad-del-carne-de-vacunacion-contra-fiebre-amarilla/) |
| Bolivia | CDC's broad table and the UK's route-specific entry page differ on how broadly the requirement applies. Refer the traveler to the Bolivian mission for the passport and route. | [CDC Yellow Book](https://www.cdc.gov/yellow-book/hcp/travel-associated-infections-diseases/yellow-fever.html) · [UK official travel advice](https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/bolivia/entry-requirements) |
| Polio | WHO's November 2026 temporary recommendations include time-limited **departure** controls for affected residents and longer-stay travelers. Verify local implementation and check any separate destination entry requirements. | [WHO November 2026 statement](https://www.who.int/cookislands/news/detail-global/13-11-2026-statement-of-the-forty-sixth-meeting-of-the-polio-ihr-emergency-committee) |
| Saudi pilgrimage | Check the Saudi Ministry of Health's season-specific Hajj or Umrah requirements for meningococcal, polio and yellow fever proof. General tourism rules must be checked separately. | [Saudi Ministry of Health](https://www.moh.gov.sa/en/healthawareness/pilgrims-health/pages/default.aspx) |

## African destination candidates for direct confirmation

CDC's table identifies Ghana and 18 additional African destinations for all-origin yellow fever certificate screening. The additional candidates are Angola, Benin, Burkina Faso, Burundi, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Côte d’Ivoire, Democratic Republic of the Congo, Gabon, Guinea, Guinea-Bissau, Mali, Niger, Republic of the Congo, Sierra Leone, South Sudan, Togo and Uganda. The table was last reviewed in September 2024. Confirm with each destination's current guidance before treating a candidate as a published app rule, especially for age limits and exceptions. [CDC screening table](https://www.cdc.gov/yellow-book/hcp/travel-associated-infections-diseases/yellow-fever.html)
