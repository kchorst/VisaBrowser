# Vaccination proof for travelers to Europe and North America — 19 November 2026

This pass prioritizes African, Asian, and South and Central American passport profiles and short visits. Record visa-application, border, departure, and transit checks at the stage where they apply. Routine vaccination advice and immigrant medical requirements are outside this pass.

| Route or destination | What to report |
|---|---|
| Romania | The foreign ministry lists people from Afghanistan, Madagascar, Pakistan and Yemen for polio vaccination and requires the international certificate when covered travelers apply for a Romanian entry visa. Ask the Romanian mission how the rule applies when the passport and place of residence or departure differ. The FAQ separately recommends vaccination for other listed origins. Keep that advice out of the required field. [Romanian eVisa FAQ](https://eviza.mae.ro/FAQ) |
| Italy | The Kolkata consulate's older notice explicitly covers residents and travelers staying more than four weeks in seven named countries, including Kenya, DRC, Nigeria, Somalia, Afghanistan, Pakistan and Syria. A Pakistan visa-centre checklist also asks for proof, while the latest embassy publishes its own application checklists. Confirm the current issuing post, checklist and travel history before displaying a mandatory flag. [Italian consular notice](https://conscalcutta.esteri.it/en/news/dal_consolato/2018/07/avviso-per-i-richiedenti-il-visto-2/) · [Islamabad embassy](https://ambislamabad.esteri.it/en/servizi-consolari-e-visti/servizi-per-il-cittadino-straniero/visti/dove-chiedere-un-visto/) |
| Pakistan departure | Pakistan's polio program instructs covered travelers to have proof at departure. This check follows the traveler and itinerary even if no certificate is needed at the destination. WHO guidance distinguishes residents and longer-stay visitors and varies by poliovirus category and local implementation. [Pakistan certificate guidance](https://www.poliofreepakistan.gov.pk/certificate/vaccination-certificate) · [WHO November notice](https://www.who.int/cookislands/news/detail-global/13-11-2026-statement-of-the-forty-sixth-meeting-of-the-polio-ihr-emergency-committee) |
| United States | The tourist requirements reviewed do not list routine vaccine proof. State Department guidance ended COVID-19 vaccine proof for nonimmigrant air travelers in May 2023. The bundled Angola, Cameroon and Gabon rows still say it is required. Flag them for correction. A separate November 2026 Ebola order applies to specified travelers with recent physical presence in DRC, Uganda or South Sudan. Refer to CDC if relevant. [Visitor visa](https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/us-visas/tourism-visit/visitor.html) · [COVID-19 change](https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/News/visas-news/update-on-change-to-us-travel-policy-requiring-covid-19-vaccination-for-nonimmigrant-travel.html) · [CDC Ebola order](https://www.cdc.gov/port-health/legal-authorities/evdorder.html) |
| Canada, Mexico, UK, Ireland and Schengen generally | No routine nationality-based vaccine certificate found in the general visitor sources reviewed. Canada and Ireland expressly removed their COVID-19 arrival proof requirements. Mexico's embassy confirms no health or vaccination requirements for visits. The Schengen Borders Code allows public-health checks and individual countries can publish specific rules. This is general evidence, with country-specific and temporary controls still to verify. [Canada](https://travel.gc.ca/travel-covid) · [Mexico](https://embamex.sre.gob.mx/chile/index.php/servicios-consulares/visas) · [UK border guidance](https://www.gov.uk/uk-border-control/before-you-leave-for-the-uk) · [Ireland](https://www.gov.ie/en/department-of-health/press-releases/ending-of-covid-19-requirements-for-travellers-to-ireland/) · [Schengen](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/EN/legal-content/summary/rules-on-crossing-eu-borders.html) |
| Jamaica and Bahamas | Route-based yellow fever proof applies to covered arrivals from risk countries. Jamaica also includes transit over 12 hours. Jamaica separately lists a passport-specific example for Kazakhstan, a tourist waiver conditioned on a qualifying foreign visa and proof of measles, rubella and polio vaccination. Keep that condition attached to the waiver route. [Jamaica Health](https://www.moh.gov.jm/jamaican-travellers-reminded-to-get-yellow-fever-vaccine/) · [Jamaica immigration](https://www.pica.gov.jm/immigration/entry-visa-requirements) · [Bahamas](https://www.bahamas.com/plan-your-trip/island-faq/visa-immigration) |
| Dominican Republic | The official tourism guidance specifies yellow fever proof for arrivals from named Brazilian states. The bundled Pakistan-to-Dominican Republic warning appears unconditional. Check for a passport-specific basis with the mission before using or removing that warning. [Dominican entry guidance](https://www.godominicanrepublic.com/travel/entry-requirements) |

In v0.4.7, Pakistan is the only bundled Romania row with a polio certificate note. The Romania finding calls for checking coverage for Afghanistan, Madagascar and Yemen. Verify remaining European countries and North American territories individually before claiming comprehensive coverage. This pass records findings only.
