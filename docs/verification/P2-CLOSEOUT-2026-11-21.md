# P2 screening closeout — November 21, 2026

The original P2 queue contains **5,871 unique passport–destination pairs**. [Issue #26](https://github.com/kchorst/VisaBrowser/issues/26) logs the first **3,755** pairs. The [closeout ledger](P2-CLOSEOUT-ROWS-2026-11-21.csv) covers the other **2,116** at passport–destination level. Counts are screening dispositions for the flagged questions.

- **1,663 scoped:** a destination or governing regime source establishes a shared condition or gives the responsible authority’s route. Verify the exact passport and listed checks before implementing a blanket classification.
- **453 refer:** the exact rule remains open. The original queue reason identifies what to ask, and the consular directory is a contact referral. Keep these records in confirmation status until better evidence is available.

No remaining P2 row lacks a disposition. The ledger preserves both the original score and every original reason code. All findings are pending the consolidated implementation. No published visa data is changed.

## Row-level questions

- `fee-needs-current-verification` — Check the actual fee, currency, payment timing and exemptions.
- `free-with-entry-steps` — Check any authorization or paperwork required alongside visa-free admission.
- `high-consequence-entry-restriction` — Verify the scope of a potential advance-visa or admission restriction.
- `route-or-residency-condition` — Check the arrival or transit route, residence condition, or crossing.
- `special-passport-document` — Check recognition and conditions for this exact passport or travel document.
- `time-sensitive-description` — Check effective dates, quotas or temporary extensions before applying the rule.
- `unreviewed-document-exception` — Check the document issuer, type, validity and any prior-use requirement.
