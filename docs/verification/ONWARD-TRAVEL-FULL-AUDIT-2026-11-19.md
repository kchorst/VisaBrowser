# Onward-travel proof by destination — 19 November 2026

**Coverage:** 275 selectable VisaBrowser destinations. The scan compares existing app text, UK Foreign Office entry pages and a secondary onward-ticket index used as a lead. It also spot-checks immigration, embassy and legal sources. UK advisories usually describe British passport travel, so mentions do not automatically apply to all passports. Each destination has a finding or a referral. Absence of a mention does not establish an exemption.

- Downloaded and searched entry guidance from 226 UK Foreign Office pages. 214 app destinations matched an entry section and 229 matched at least one external index.
- Scanned all 46,929 imported passport rules for wording about exit, return or onward tickets. Found 278 matching rows across 57 destinations. A mention can apply to a visa application, transit, boarding or border admission, or be outdated.
- Results: 44 targeted or shared legal findings, 37 other UK advisory mentions, 80 secondary leads that still need official verification, and 114 unresolved or coverage gaps.
- Scope: short-stay tourism on ordinary passports, single- and multiple-entry routes, and transit as it affects a tourist journey.

## How VisaBrowser should show this

Show confirmed check-in and border requirements in the main result. Where the condition depends on route, identify that route and keep the rule specific to it. Identify accepted proof, the exit deadline, whether the next country must admit the traveler and when a carrier can ask. Check each new entry. When the source is unclear or available only through a secondary index, provide a confirmation prompt and source. See the [initial onward-travel checks](ONWARD-TRAVEL-2026-11-19.md). This ledger does not change live results.
