# VisaBrowser — Phase 1 priority audit and Phase 2 official verification

**Reviewed:** 2026-11-19 · **Baseline app:** v0.4.6 · **Scope:** every passport profile and passport–destination source record.

## Phase 1: reproducible risk-ranked queue

The complete source/code audit covers **207 passport profiles**, **202 populated source files**, **46,929 unique passport–destination rows**, and **five unavailable territorial-source profiles**: `X-ABK`, `X-NCY`, `X-SOM`, `X-SOS`, `X-TRN`.

The initial screening (see [audit issue #18](https://github.com/kchorst/VisaBrowser/issues/18)) identified **742** records with electronic-authorization source labels that trigger the generic pending-verification fallback and **1,487** visa-free records with a condition or administrative flag. Those counts overlap. The source also contains **1,563 unreviewed document-exception entries**. They are *candidates*, not automatically wrong or safe.

The new `scripts/verification_queue.py` scans **every imported row**, assigns **one unique risk priority per passport/destination pair**, and records the reasons, source snapshot date, and source URL. It sorts the queue deterministically and produces:

- `verification-queue-all.csv`: every passport–destination record, prioritized
- `verification-queue-top500.csv`: immediate review shortlist
- `verification-summary.json`: priority counts, unavailable sources, and hotspots

Run `python scripts/verification_queue.py --output audit-output` locally, or download the `VisaBrowser-verification-queue` artifact from the **VisaBrowser verification priority audit** GitHub Action. The CSV is a queue for human/official-source verification, not an automatically rewritten visa table.

**Measured ranking (19 Nov 2026):** **P0: 15 pairs**, **P1: 2,930 pairs**, **P2: 5,871 pairs**, **P3: 38,113 pairs** — together exactly **46,929 unique pairs**. The five unavailable passport profiles appear in the summary separately rather than as fictitious entry rules. The P0 queue includes existing time-bound protection for Kuwait–NZ alongside the 14 newly identified corrections.

### Priority definitions

| Tier | Rationale | Disposition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **P0** | Already identified as conflicting with or requiring urgent protection under official government guidance | Verify and add narrowly scoped reviewed override before next application release |
| **P1** | Potential misleading visa/authorization label, unclassified eligibility, serious contradiction, or other high-impact rule | Official nationality- and route-level verification |
| **P2** | Conditional visa-free travel, fees, temporary dates, exemptions, route/health/passport requirements | Document authority, effective date, exemptions, and safe display classification |
| **P3** | No triggering risk term; still subject to independent verification | Routine sampling and deferred source review |

Scores are heuristic. **No candidate is promoted to visa-free merely because its wording resembles an ETA.**

## Phase 2: checked official sources and specific discrepancies

The machine-readable ledger is [official-findings-2026-11-19.json](official-findings-2026-11-19.json). It captures **17 directly checked examples**, including **14 identified new discrepancies** requiring scoped corrections and **3 already addressed by v0.4.6 and retained for monitoring**. This is *not* verification of all 46,929 rules.

### New discrepancies requiring the next controlled correction release

1. **United Kingdom — British National (Overseas) passport (`BNO → GB`).** VisaBrowser's imported source remains unclassified ("Bespoke UK Immigration Status"), producing a misleading uncertain result. The UK explicitly exempts BN(O) passport visitors from both a visitor visa and ETA, subject to ordinary immigration admission. [UK government: when an ETA is not needed](https://www.gov.uk/eta/when-not-need-eta).
2. **Kenya — nine eTA-exempt passport sources contain eTA labels or flags.** Official nationality lists exempt **Republic of the Congo (`CG`), Gambia (`GM`), St. Lucia (`LC`), Mozambique (`MZ`)** for visits not exceeding **90 days**; and **Cameroon (`CM`), Cape Verde (`CV`), Seychelles (`SC`), Senegal (`SN`), São Tomé and Príncipe (`ST`)** for visits not exceeding **60 days**. Their imported records wrongly imply eTA paperwork. [Kenya Directorate of Immigration: exemption lists](https://etakenya.go.ke/form/apply/how-to-apply). This is a **narrow nationality exemption**, not a new blanket visa-free rule for every passport holder.
3. **Kenya — four East African Partner State source records show narrower stays than the official exemption window.** Imported `BI` (Burundi), `CD` (DR Congo), `SS` (South Sudan), and `TZ` (Tanzania) show 90 days/3 months, whereas Kenya's published EAC exemption states **not exceeding 180 days**. Correct the description of the *exemption window*, not a guarantee of the actual stay granted on arrival. [Kenya Directorate of Immigration](https://etakenya.go.ke/form/apply/how-to-apply).

### Existing v0.4.6 reviewed exceptions requiring monitoring

- `KW → NZ`: Kuwaiti visa-waiver suspension from 12 Nov 2026 until 11 Feb 2027 (New Zealand time), with a time-bound reviewed override. [Immigration New Zealand](https://www.immigration.govt.nz/about-us/news-centre/visa-waiver-travel-changes-for-kuwait-nationals/).
- `US → GB`: UK ETA requirement and £20 application fee; already reviewed. [GOV.UK ETA](https://www.gov.uk/eta).
- `US → KR`: K-ETA temporary exemption through 31 Dec 2026 (Korean time); already reviewed, expiring soon. [Korea Immigration Service](https://www.k-eta.go.kr/portal/board/viewboarddetail.do?bbsSn=299707&locale=EN).

### Rules for corrections

Verify the **exact passport type**, nationality, tourism purpose, arrival method, maximum exemption period, application fee, government page, source checked date, and any expiry date. If the official rule is unclear or newly changed, remain **Needs Review**, with a meaningful reason. Do not erase warnings unrelated to visa status (health, passport validity, admission conditions). In particular, preserve the app's strict **NO ISSUES** standard.

## What has not been done

- No government-by-government verification of all 46,929 rules.
- No automatic alteration of imported visa files or live application classifications in this audit phase.
- No assertion that the five missing passport source tables are covered.
- No claim of Windows end-user acceptance.

**Next engineering gate:** implement the 14 new authority-backed corrections in one versioned release; test the corrected passport/destination matrix and issue a consolidated release only after validation and review.
