# Vaccination-proof audit beyond yellow fever — 19 November 2026

Scope includes short visits, destination and departure checks, and special pathways when useful to travelers. The screening ledger covers each of the 275 catalog destinations. We compared the WHO global country list's additional-requirement entries with targeted searches of government, aviation, public health and consular sources. This is a global screening pass with targeted source checks. An entry with no finding is not a separately verified exemption. Check current destination requirements and stops before departure. [WHO country list, revised January 2023](https://cdn.who.int/media/docs/default-source/travel-and-health/vaccination-requirements-and-who-recommendations-ith-2022-country-list.pdf) · [WHO November 2026 polio notice](https://www.who.int/cookislands/news/detail-global/13-11-2026-statement-of-the-forty-sixth-meeting-of-the-polio-ihr-emergency-committee)

## Findings retained or referred

- **Romania and Italy.** Keep the Romanian polio requirement for covered visa applicants from Afghanistan, Madagascar, Pakistan and Yemen. Verify with the mission when passport, residence and route differ. Italy's older notice is a consular checklist referral. [Romania](https://eviza.mae.ro/FAQ) · [Italy](https://conscalcutta.esteri.it/en/news/dal_consolato/2018/07/avviso-per-i-richiedenti-il-visto-2/)
- **Older polio entries.** Bangladesh (arriving children under five), Brunei, Georgia, Iran, Saint Kitts and Nevis, Morocco, Maldives, Peru, and Qatar have dated records. They remain prompts pending confirmation. Afghanistan and Solomon Islands appear in departure or WHO temporary-measure material. India publishes its own port health advisory. [WHO list](https://www.who.int/publications/m/item/countries-with-risk-of-yellow-fever-transmission-and-countries-requiring-yellow-fever-vaccination-(november-2022)) · [Afghanistan travel advice](https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/afghanistan/entry-requirements) · [India port health](https://www.ihpoe.mohfw.gov.in/)
- **Departure and pilgrimage records.** Afghanistan, Bangladesh, Denmark, Indonesia, Jordan, Libya, Maldives, Niger, Peru and Syria have older notes on additional proof for travel from their territory. The old entries mention meningococcal, influenza, polio or Haemophilus influenzae proof in different contexts. Verify the origin-country guidance and current Saudi pilgrimage season before showing a mandatory notice.
- **China, Brazil, Japan, Taiwan and the U.S.** China already has a route-based yellow fever check in the draft. Brazil’s health ministry states no general vaccination-proof requirement for entry. Japan ended COVID-19 proof and CDC lists no yellow fever requirement for Taiwan. U.S. immigrant visas have their own vaccination rules, outside this tourist comparison. U.S. outbreak restrictions are separate from vaccine proof. [China](https://wwwnc.cdc.gov/travel/destinations/traveler/none/China) · [Brazil](https://www.gov.br/saude/pt-br/vacinacao/viajantes/vacinacao-para-os-viajantes) · [Japan](https://www.mhlw.go.jp/stf/covid-19/bordercontrol.html) · [Taiwan](https://wwwnc.cdc.gov/travel/destinations/traveler/none/Taiwan) · [U.S. immigrant visas](https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/us-visas/immigrate/vaccinations.html)
- **Advice-only vaccines.** Measles, rabies and encephalitis recommendations go outside required proof unless an authority specifies an applicable entry or visa rule. CDC reports no country entry requirement for cholera vaccination. [PAHO measles alert](https://www.paho.org/sites/default/files/2026/02/2026-feb-3-phe-alerta-epi-measles-enfinal1.pdf) · [CDC Yellow Book on cholera](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK620863/)
