# VisaBrowser vaccination certificate audit — full destination ledger

**Checked 19 November 2026.** All 275 selectable destinations are accounted for. The historical WHO country list matched 217 of them: 118 reported a yellow fever certificate rule and 99 reported none. 58 destinations had no separately matched WHO entry. These are screening totals from the historical list. Later sources and exceptions can change the result. A missing source or a historical “no” does not establish a current exemption. The ledger also includes additional checks for polio, pilgrimage certificates and selected obsolete COVID-19 text.

## How the app should apply the findings

- **All origins:** Show the certificate in the destination result for every covered traveler. Apply age and exceptions from the jurisdiction. Ghana is the priority example.
- **Specific routes:** Show a visible “Check your recent travel and transit” notice if the user has entered only a passport. Use actual origin and transit with the destination’s accepted country list. Preserve age thresholds, lookback windows, named subregions and exemptions. Do not label African, Asian or American passport holders as a group as requiring a certificate.
- **Specific passports, residency and visa pathways:** Attach a requirement to the exact pathway only. Romania names Afghanistan, Madagascar, Pakistan and Yemen for its polio rule. Jamaica ties measles, rubella and polio proof to selected tourist visa waivers. Kazakhstan is one in the requested passport groups. [Romanian MFA](https://eviza.mae.ro/FAQ) · [Jamaica Immigration](https://www.pica.gov.jm/immigration/entry-visa-requirements)
- **Departure and other stages:** Pakistan polio proof belongs on a departure notice for covered travelers. Paraguay has a separate yellow fever departure rule. WHO’s temporary polio recommendations apply by transmission category, residence or longer stays, and local implementation. [Pakistan](https://www.poliofreepakistan.gov.pk/certificate/vaccination-certificate) · [Paraguay](https://dgvs.mspbs.gov.py/requisitos-sanitarios-para-el-ingreso-a-paraguay/) · [WHO November 2026 polio statement](https://www.who.int/cookislands/news/detail-global/13-11-2026-statement-of-the-forty-sixth-meeting-of-the-polio-ihr-emergency-committee)
- **Origin screening:** These country codes give a starting list for recent yellow fever exposure checks, drawn from CDC’s risk tables. Use the destination’s own list for actual matching. Risk can be limited to specific regions, and transit triggers vary. Codes: `AO BJ BF BI CM CF TD CG CD CI GQ ET GA GM GH GN GW KE LR ML MR NE NG SN SL SS SD TG UG AR BO BR CO EC GF GY PA PY PE SR TT VE`. [CDC Yellow Book risk table](https://www.cdc.gov/yellow-book/hcp/travel-associated-infections-diseases/yellow-fever.html)
- **Warnings and referrals:** Show what to confirm if the source is old, conflicting, or absent. For a territory, check the specific island, region, entry point or administering jurisdiction. [Consular contacts](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/foreign-embassies-in-the-uk)
- **Existing app findings:** The scan of all 46,929 imported rows and the focused checks for European and North American routes remain available in the [initial audit](VACCINATION-ENTRY-AUDIT-2026-11-19.md) and [regional audit](VACCINATION-EUROPE-NORTH-AMERICA-2026-11-19.md). In particular, remove stale COVID-19 proof warnings for Angola, Cameroon and Gabon passports entering the U.S. when implementation is approved.

## Origin-specific additions for implementation

| Affected passport, residence or recent route | Add to VisaBrowser |
|---|---|
| Afghanistan, Madagascar, Pakistan and Yemen → Romania | Polio certificate for covered Romanian visa applicants. Confirm with the consulate where citizenship, residence and departure differ. |
| Afghanistan, DRC, Kenya, Nigeria, Pakistan, Somalia and Syria → Italy | The older Italian notice covers residents and stays over four weeks. Show a consular checklist referral until the issuing post confirms the current list. |
| Leaving Pakistan | Departure certificate warning for covered travelers regardless of onward destination. |
| Kazakhstan → Jamaica using the listed waiver | Measles, rubella and polio certificate attached to that waiver and qualifying third-country visa. |
| Recent yellow fever risk area → Malta, Singapore, Jamaica, Bahamas and other listed destinations | Ask about departure country and all connections. Show the vaccination certificate warning only when the destination’s itinerary condition applies, or show a check-route prompt if the itinerary is unknown. |
| Covered parts of Brazil → Dominican Republic; Brazil → Venezuela | Show route-specific yellow fever proof and preserve the distinction between the Dominican Republic’s named states and Venezuela’s Brazil rule. |
| Covered areas of Bolivia, Brazil, Colombia, Guyana or Peru → Paraguay | Show the local age, region and certificate rules. Check applicable departure rules independently. |
| Any passport → Ghana | Show the destination-wide certificate requirement for covered arrivals regardless of origin. |
| Angola, Cameroon and Gabon passports → U.S. | Remove outdated mandatory COVID-19 proof. Any current public health entry order is a separate issue. |
