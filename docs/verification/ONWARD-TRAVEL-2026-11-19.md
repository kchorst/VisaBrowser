# Onward travel proof — initial tourist-entry checks

Checked November 19, 2026. Scope is ordinary passport holders on short tourist trips, including single- and multiple-entry routes.

| Destination and pathway | What travelers should have ready | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Costa Rica, nonresident visitors | Proof of departure before the visa or entry stamp expires. Official visitor guidance lists prepaid bus or flight tickets and proof of cruise passage. This can matter even when the traveler flies home from another country. | [Costa Rica entry requirements](https://www.visitcostarica.com/planning-your-trip/entry-requirements) |
| New Zealand, visa-waiver visitors | A paid ticket out before travel. Immigration also accepts specified evidence for cruises and private craft. A further onward ticket may be requested if the next destination also requires it. | [Immigration New Zealand visa waiver visitor guidance](https://www.immigration.govt.nz/visas/visa-waiver-visitor-visa/) |
| Maldives, tourist visa on arrival | A complete itinerary normally includes confirmed return tickets, accommodation and sufficient funds. Immigration also lists an approved sponsorship route. | [Maldives Immigration tourist visa](https://www.immigration.gov.mv/visa/tourist-visa) |
| Philippines, tourist arrivals | The Bureau of Immigration lists a return or onward ticket in its tourist arrival checklist. Confirm exceptions and applicable visa pathway. | [Bureau of Immigration Citizens' Charter](https://www.immigration.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/BUREAU-OF-IMMIGRATION-CITIZENS-CHARTER-2025-1ST-EDITION.pdf) |

App addition to audit: a visible “Proof of onward travel” item whenever the source confirms a requirement. Record when it is checked, what forms of transportation count, the departure deadline, and whether the traveler must show eligibility to enter the next country. Distinguish a confirmed paid ticket from other acceptable evidence and from a discretionary request. Treat each new entry as a separate check, including after a side trip on a multiple-entry visa.

Current app inspection found onward travel in some New Zealand guidance and China transit checks. This first batch is not a destination-wide inventory and changes no app behavior.
