# Release notes v0.4.15

November 26, 2026

Open **Border and baggage rules** on destination results and itinerary cards for 13 new sourced notes covering Canada, Malaysia, the Netherlands, Poland and Saudi Arabia. The visa eligibility dataset is unchanged.

| Destination | Changes |
| --- | --- |
| Canada | Quarantine Act disclosure and immigration medical examinations for qualifying applicants. |
| Malaysia | Vaping, tobacco and alcohol allowances, and restrictions on covered textiles and media. |
| Netherlands | Declaring qualifying cultural goods such as old books and documents when entering from outside the EU. |
| Poland | Restrictions on importing covered material for distribution. |
| Saudi Arabia | Passenger plant items, pets, protected wildlife, media, tobacco and re-export of registered antiquities. |

Canada’s near-expiry temporary border order and Malaysia’s provisional eastern Sabah maritime guidance remain in the research queue. Source links, jurisdiction and check dates appear with the published notes.
